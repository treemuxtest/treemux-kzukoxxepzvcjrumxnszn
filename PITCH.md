GrantPilot is the first grant intelligence cockpit built for grassroots operators who need premium funding without pausing delivery. It ingests a founder’s mission, traction, and constraints once, then scores live grant programs with a transparent rules engine and an AI copilot that writes funder-ready briefs in seconds. Radar visualizations, deadline pipelines, and clipboard-ready prep kits make the app demo-ready on stage and deployment-ready in the field. With one click, teams walk into TreeHacks judging with a quantified funding strategy instead of a wish list.
